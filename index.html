About Us

Tradition since 2010

Burger started in 2010, when Mr. Smith opened a small kitchen with one grill and a short menu. We still shape every patty by hand, bake our buns each morning and build every burger to order.

We only use seasonal ingredients, so the menu shifts a little with every harvest.
